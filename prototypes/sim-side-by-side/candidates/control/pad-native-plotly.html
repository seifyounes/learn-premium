For the Owner

Real: every curve and number comes from control-core.engine.mjs, the same core as the root-locus candidate, checked in Node by check-control-core.mjs (31 of 31 PASS against python-control: PM 51.827° at 1.5723 rad/s, OS 16.303 %, poles −1 ± 1.732051j). Phase is summed factor by factor, so it never wraps. Plotly only draws; the red pen (PM bracket, ring at crossover, overshoot and settling marks) is drawn as our own traces.

First load: measuring…. Plotly's bundle is 4.7 MB unzipped and this local server does not gzip (about 1.4 MB gzipped). Touch: works: tap a curve for a value readout; zoom and pan are switched off so the page scrolls; on a phone the three views sit behind printed tabs.

The python-control button is here only so you feel its cost on a phone: the research puts python-control in the build gate, not in the page. On this laptop it took about 26 s and about 33 MB (Pyodide, NumPy, SciPy, matplotlib and friends, python-control), and agreed with our core on all six values to within 3 × 10⁻⁵.
