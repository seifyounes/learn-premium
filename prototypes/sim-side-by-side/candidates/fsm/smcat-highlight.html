For the Owner

  • Real: the diagram is state-machine-cat 12 (graphviz dot layout) rendering an agent-written text file (machine-101.smcat) once, in Node (build-smcat.mjs): one left-right SVG for wide screens, one top-down for phones. The page only re-colours it and toggles classes; a 5-line lookup engine moves the highlight.
  • Accuracy: check-smcat-highlight.mjs parses the smcat source with smcat itself and runs the tiny engine: state table and test sequence, 21 of 21 PASS.
  • Faked or missing: no motion along arrows, no tables, no timing strip. The state boxes keep smcat's rounded corners (it draws them as paths; the design system wants square). Arrow positions come from graphviz, so they can change when the machine changes.
  • First load: 169 KB cold from the local server, about 140 KB of it the pad fonts; no layout engine loads in the browser (the SVGs are 8 to 9 KB).
  • Touch: works; the controls are 52 px tall and nothing needs hover (a CDP touch tap on CLOCK clocks it). On a phone the top-down diagram is tall, so S3 sits below the fold.
