For the Owner

  • Real: this is the current CircuitJS build (self-hosted copy in vendor/circuitjs/), exactly as it ships: its own dark canvas, menus, sidebar, scopes and editing. Each circuit is the file from circuits/ loaded with ?cct=; the buck uses its steady-state-seeded text so it does not spend 25 ms of simulated time settling.
  • Accuracy: not re-checked here. The circuit-sim prototype measured these same files in CircuitJS: buck Vo 19.52 V (key 20, the diode drop), SCR Vo,av 40.18 V (key 40.5, its fixed ~0.9 V SCR drop), CE amp Av −157 (key −163.3, CircuitJS's VT is 25.9 mV). Candidate B's "real devices" mode runs these same files and measured 19.51 V and 40.21 V live. There is no idealisation switch here: CircuitJS always uses real-ish devices.
  • Weight: measured on a cold load in headless Chrome: 1.31 MB transferred in 38 requests, fonts included. The sidebar's "Error response 404" is this self-hosted copy missing CircuitJS's example-circuit library; the simulator itself is unaffected.
  • Touch: partial. CircuitJS has its own touch handling and a phone menu button, but its menus, long-press edit dialogs, small scope strip and element hit targets are desktop-first. Checked only in an emulated 390 px viewport, not on a real phone.
  • To ship: nothing to build, but it looks like CircuitJS, not the pad, and a student can edit or break the circuit freely.
