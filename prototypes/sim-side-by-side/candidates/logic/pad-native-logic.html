For the Owner

  • Real: every value comes from a small gate-level engine (pad-native-logic.engine.mjs), not from animation. Ideal mode evaluates the gates in logic-depth order; Real devices runs an event-driven simulation with a delay per gate. check-pad-native-logic.mjs runs it in Node against circuits/expected.json: all 8 rows, all 64 row-to-row changes in both modes, and the Cout hazard timing: 20/20 PASS. The same checks passed in the browser.
  • Assumed: the real-device delays (XOR 12 ns, AND 8 ns, OR 8 ns) are illustrative placeholders of the right order for a 74HC-class gate, not measured or from a datasheet; the model uses transport delay (short pulses are not swallowed). The schematic coordinates are hand-placed for this one circuit.
  • Weight: about 190 KB on first load, measured in the browser: 45 KB of page, engine and CSS (uncompressed), the other 145 KB are the pad fonts every page shares. No libraries.
  • Touch: works: 60 px input switches, 44 px truth-table rows on a phone, and the wire ends in the drawing are tappable too.
  • To ship: a layout step that places gates and routes wires from a netlist (or from the Professor's figure) for any circuit; flip-flops and clocks for sequential circuits; RTL mirroring of the drawing was not attempted.
