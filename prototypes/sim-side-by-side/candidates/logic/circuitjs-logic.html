For the Owner

  • Real: the self-hosted current CircuitJS build (vendor/circuitjs/) running circuits/logic/circuitjs.txt unchanged, with the embed options hideSidebar, hideMenu, whiteBackground, editable=false. Not re-skinned. Logic here is analogue underneath: gates are 5 V models, so the strip reads node voltages (above 2.5 V = 1).
  • Weight: about 1.3 MB on first load, measured in the browser: 1.13 MB of CircuitJS scripts (about 270 KB gzipped; this dev server sends them uncompressed) plus 145 KB of pad fonts every page shares.
  • Touch: tapping a logic input toggles it (checked at phone width), but on a phone the whole drawing shrinks to fit, so the L/H inputs are about 10 px targets and the labels are unreadably small. The red padlock is CircuitJS's own sign that editing is off. It takes a few seconds to start.
  • To ship: GPL-2.0 (a licence decision); the drawing is small on a phone; the look is CircuitJS's, with its own colours inside our frame.
