For the Owner

  • Real: the unmodified app (GPL-3.0, github.com/picuino/neumatic, commit fe66ee7, 2026-08-31), self-hosted from vendor/neumatic/docs/ and loaded with its own ?loadFile=multi-abba.txt example. That example is a two-group cascade: group valve 1.5, rollers 1.2/1.3/2.2/2.3, INIT VALVE 1.4 in series with 1.2. Numbering is Picuino’s (1.0 = A, 2.0 = B), not ours.
  • Accuracy: observed, not gated. In the browser I polled its two cylinders after one INIT press: A+ B+ B− A−, then both home (a physical-ish air model, about 3.3 s per cycle). Its engine is tied to the canvas, so a Node gate would need the engine extracted first.
  • Weight: first load 183 KB: its single 166 KB index.html + the 5.7 KB example, plus this 4 KB wrapper and the shared 7.5 KB pad.css (uncompressed from the static server), then the shared pad fonts.
  • Touch: no. Confirmed on the 375 px mobile preset: the canvas binds only mousedown/mousemove/mouseup (no touch or pointer handlers), it renders 500 px wide so cylinder B and the group valve are cut off, and its own pan needs middle-mouse or Space-drag. A phone browser’s compatibility click might still fire the push-button on a tap (not tried on a device); swiping the frame sideways is the only way to see the rest.
  • To ship: add pointer events, strip the editor chrome (File/Draw/Edit menus) for a student view, re-skin to the pad, extract the engine for a Node gate, and replace coordinate-matched pipes with port-named connections.
