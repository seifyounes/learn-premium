Why a link, not a frame

MechSimulator cannot be embedded: it sends frame-ancestors 'self' and X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN (checked today), and its terms forbid copying or mirroring. So it opens in its own tab, as a look-and-feel benchmark to hold the other two candidates against.

Open MechSimulator

There: tap Cascade under Pre-built circuits, then Run Circuit, then the 3/2 Push Button in the diagram. It shows a cookie notice for analytics and ads.

What it does well (seen 2026-09-26)
  1. The same circuit is one tap away. Its Cascade preset loads exactly A+ B+ B− A−, with a short plain-language paragraph on why the group valve cures the overlap (group 1 exhausts, so a1 stops holding B+).
  2. Air you can see. Lines animate as pressure moves, working lines are solid and pilot lines dashed, and a 0–6 bar colour scale sits under the diagram. Valve symbols switch position as they are piloted.
  3. Numbers beside the picture. Live tiles for supply pressure, flow, air used per cycle, net cylinder force, piston speed and load ratio: a semi-physical model (force balance, orifice flow), not just a sequencer.
  4. A whole course in one tool. Simulate / Explore / Practice / Quiz modes, electro-pneumatic and hydraulic siblings, 52 drag-and-drop ISO 1219 parts, zoom and pan buttons, share links.
  5. Where it is weaker: the look is a dark glowing dashboard (the opposite of the pad); on a phone it asks you to drag the diagram sideways or use a desktop; and with the cascade loaded its displacement-diagram header still read “A+ B+ A− B− sequence”.

For the Owner

  • Real vs faked: nothing of MechSimulator is in the repo: no screenshot, no code, no copied text. This page is a link plus my notes from using it in a browser tab today.
  • Weight: this page is 5 KB plus the shared pad.css and fonts. MechSimulator’s own weight is not measured (it loads outside our frame, with analytics and ad scripts).
  • Touch: the link works on touch. MechSimulator itself binds pointer/touch and pinch-zoom per the research; on a phone it recommends a larger screen.
  • Accuracy: not checked against our key. I watched its cascade start (A+) after pressing its push button; I did not time or verify the full order there.
